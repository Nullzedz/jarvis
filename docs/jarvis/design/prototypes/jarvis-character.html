JARVIS · character sheet

Design A, "the object": a matte white body, light from above, and a real slot cut into him where the blue eye lives and moves. No glow, no glass. His life comes from where he looks, how he leans and how he blinks.

Anatomy

The proportions everything is built from, at the reference size of 140 × 98.

Body140 × 98, corner 34 (a soft squircle) Material#f6f7f9 top to #dcdfe4 bottom, matte Top edgea 1.5 px white line, the light catching his top Eye slot88 × 20, corner 10, cut into him: #0b0f17 inside, a 1 px rim Eye#0a84ff bar 64 × 10, always inside the slot Shadowa faint contact patch under him, nothing else Neverglows, halos, gradients on the eye, glass, outlines in colour Moods changethe eye's colour, length and place, his tilt, never his shape

Turnaround

He's a real object about a third as deep as he is wide, so he can turn in the island and on the desktop.

Sizes

From the front page down to a window's header icon; the slot and eye stay readable at every size.
