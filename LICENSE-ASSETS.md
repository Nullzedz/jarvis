# JARVIS — name, character and artwork

JARVIS's code is under the [MIT License](LICENSE). Its name, its character (the white visor with the blue eye and his halo), its icon and its interface design are JARVIS's own.

## Where JARVIS comes from

JARVIS is built on [Coucou](https://github.com/Louis-CFM/coucou) by Louis Raillé: its code was the backbone all of this grew from, and its MIT copyright notice is kept in [LICENSE](LICENSE). JARVIS has since gone its own way, in design and in what it does. It doesn't use Coucou's name or its Mochi character.

The macOS app (`NotchBuddy/`) and the Android app (`android/`) came with Coucou and are kept here as they were adapted. The Android app's own notices ([android/LICENSE](android/LICENSE), [android/PERMISSION.md](android/PERMISSION.md)) still apply to it.
